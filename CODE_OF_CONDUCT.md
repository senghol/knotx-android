# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the KnotX project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions regardless of experience, background, nationality, gender, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming constructive feedback.
- Accepting different opinions and approaches.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Helping others learn and contribute.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately provoking others.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Threatening or abusive language.
- Any other behaviour considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project maintainers privately.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions related to KnotX.
